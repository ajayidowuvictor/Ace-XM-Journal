# ACE XM JOURNAL: Brand

Source: logo supplied by the owner, stored at `assets/brand/acexm-logo.jpg` (ACEXm wordmark with bull and candlestick emblem).

## Colours (sampled from the logo file)

| Token    | Hex       | Use                                             |
| -------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| navy     | `#2D3684` | Primary brand colour, headers, primary buttons  |
| green    | `#43B54B` | Success, progress, positive actions             |
| red      | `#E92026` | Small accent only (alerts, destructive actions) |
| charcoal | `#474444` | Tagline grey, secondary text                    |

Dark-mode variants and accessible contrast pairs will be defined with the design tokens in Phase 2 and checked for readable contrast.

## Notes

- The app name is always written **ACE XM JOURNAL**.
- The logo is the owner's own and is used as supplied. The app icon will be derived from the bull emblem so it stays legible at small sizes.
